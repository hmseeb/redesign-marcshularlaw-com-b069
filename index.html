How We Help

Family law matters we handle

Whatever your family's situation, our team brings knowledge and compassion to your unique case.

Divorce

Divorce is usually handled by a divorce lawyer, but if your family lawyer has experience in the field he can help. We look out for your best interests — getting you your share of property, helping you maintain custody of your children, and working out alimony.

Legal Separation

Sometimes you want time apart from your spouse without necessarily getting a divorce. We help make clear who is entitled to what and when, and make sure both parties stick to it.

Child Custody

Custody matters can be very difficult. Engage the services of a family lawyer early — they will fight to ensure your wishes are honored where child custody is concerned.

Child Support

Once you have a child with someone they are required to help bring up the child. We follow up with the other parent to make sure they meet their obligation.

Division of Property

Property is a sensitive issue. We assess your situation, let you know what you can and cannot get, and argue on your behalf either before your family or in court.

Spousal Support

Legally, your spouse may be required to continue to offer you the same kind of life through spousal support. We help make sure you get what you deserve.
